Common Questions

Recruiter & HR Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about setting up JobSnap for your hiring team.

How does JobSnap calculate the AI match score?

JobSnap compares verified technical skills, domain experience, and past accomplishments directly against your requisition requirements to compute a 0–100% objective fit score.

Can I export candidate data directly to Excel?

Yes. With 1 click on the "Export to Excel (.csv)" button, your active or filtered candidate list downloads in formatted UTF-8 BOM CSV, ready for Excel or your ATS.

How does the 6-stage hiring pipeline work?

The funnel visually tracks candidates moving from Applied → Reviewed → Shortlisted → Interview → Offer → Hired. Clicking any stage filters your applicants list instantly.

Is JobSnap free for recruiters during the beta?

Yes! During the early access beta, talent teams and recruiters can publish requisitions, screen candidates, and utilize the full dashboard without subscription fees.
