Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about AI JobSnap early access, security, and career automation.

Is AI JobSnap really 100% free right now?

Yes! All 11 core features — including multi-source scraping, AI relevance scoring, Kanban application tracking, and automated email reports — are completely free during our public beta while compute capacity allows.

How does the AI relevance scoring work?

Our intelligent heuristic engine analyzes job titles, required technical skills, years of experience, and role responsibilities against your profile and resume. Jobs receive an objective 0–100% match score so you focus on top opportunities first.

Will my current employer know I am job hunting?

Never. AI JobSnap runs in stealth mode. We do not publish candidate directories or sell data to recruiters. Your target search profiles, saved companies, and application logs are accessible only to you.

What is the Assisted Application Filling feature?

Our application assistant launches a dedicated browser session that automatically pre-populates repetitive fields (name, contact info, LinkedIn URL, work history) on standard ATS application portals, saving you hours of tedious manual typing.

How do daily email summaries and Excel sheets work?

Every morning, AI JobSnap scans your target profiles for new listings. You can configure it to send a morning brief directly to your inbox with direct apply links and an attached, beautifully formatted Excel spreadsheet tracking all new opportunities.

How does Google Calendar sync help me?

When an employer schedules a screening call or interview, or when an application deadline approaches, AI JobSnap automatically syncs the event to your Google Calendar so you never miss an interview deadline or follow-up window.
