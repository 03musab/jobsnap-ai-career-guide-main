Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about AI JobSnap early access, security, and career automation.

What are the two account roles in JobSnap?

Choose Job Seeker for job matching and roadmaps, or Recruiter & Enterprise for sourcing. Both access encrypted, role-specific consoles.

How do Fast Mode (~60s) and Deep Mode (~3m) scans differ?

Fast Mode completes in ~60 seconds across primary boards. Deep Mode scans for ~3 minutes across niche and maritime portals.

Can I search specific domains like Python, Electrical, or Merchant Navy?

Yes. Enter any keyword—such as Python, Electrical, or Merchant Navy—filter by location and job type, and save your profile for 1-click re-runs.

How does the AI Relevance Score calculate if a job is 'Perfect For You'?

JobSnap compares your parsed skills and experience with job requirements, outputting a 0–100% score and highlighting top fits as "Perfect For You".

Can I connect a separate Google account for Calendar syncing?

Yes. You can connect any Google account to sync application deadlines and refresh calendar events directly on page.

How does Email Reporting and Excel dispatch work?

Add recipient emails in console settings to automatically receive a formatted Excel sheet of all discovered jobs and match scores after each scan.

What is the Outreach Studio?

It's your friendly shortcut to real hiring teams! Instead of waiting weeks on crowded job boards, you can quickly find verified recruiters and managers, choose a warm, polite message template, and reach out directly with one click.

How does the Skill Gap & Roadmap section accelerate my career?

It diagnoses missing skills against industry benchmarks and creates step-by-step project tasks with in-page AI answers and PDF export.
