# Features & Services: Core Application Workflow

This document records the exact product workflow updates for the three cards in **Our Features & Services**:

## 1. Smart Job Discovery (Dashboard & Keyword Scanner)
- **Always-Open Scan Console**: Dedicated left-hand sidebar featuring a prominent, permanent glowing emerald **SCAN** button that crawls on demand.
- **Dynamic Keyword Filtering**:
  - Filter by specific keywords: e.g. **Python** for software engineering, **Electrical** for hardware engineering, and **Merchant Navy** for maritime positions.
  - Multi-platform scanning across LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor, and specialized company careers pages.
- **Card Visual**: An interactive dark obsidian dashboard preview displaying the left-hand SCAN trigger, the active keyword filter chips (`Python`, `Electrical`, `Merchant Navy`), and incoming discovered listings.
- **Detail Modal**: Deep dive into the multi-platform crawler architecture, keyword tokenization, and instant freshness alerts.

---

## 2. AI Resume Matching (Relevance Scoring & Perfect Fit Verdict)
- **Skill Set Extraction**: Analyzes candidate resume and extracts core competencies, tools, and technical domain certifications.
- **Calibrated Relevance Score**: Compares resume skills against requirements across scanned job descriptions, computing an objective 0–100% score (e.g., 96% Relevance).
- **"Perfect For You" Determination**: Automatically tags high-alignment openings with an unmistakable **"✓ PERFECT FOR YOU"** verdict to highlight top-priority roles.
- **Card Visual**: Dark diagnostic surface displaying the parsed resume skill set, the 96% circular relevance meter, and the glowing emerald "PERFECT FOR YOU" verdict badge.
- **Detail Modal**: Full diagnostic breakdown of missing keyword suggestions, skill weighting, and callback rate optimization.

---

## 3. 1-Click Application Hub (Discovered vs Applied Portal)
- **Workflow Pipeline Portal**:
  - **Discovered Column**: Newly crawled jobs appear here with live match tags and 1-click apply readiness.
  - **Applied Column**: Tracks successfully submitted roles with timestamps, application history, and status checkmarks.
- **1-Click Application Dispatch**: Pre-fills repetitive ATS portal questionnaires and submits candidate credentials with a single tap.
- **Card Visual**: Dual-column Kanban portal board showing active counts (`Discovered: 8`, `Applied: 14`), job preview cards, and the central 1-Click Apply automation arrow.
- **Detail Modal**: Full walkthrough of ATS auto-fill, interview sync, and pipeline velocity.
